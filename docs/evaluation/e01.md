# Frozen E01 resident timing protocol

This zero-provider **technical platform assay** is separate from gameplay,
P13 volume and ordinary local-player acceptance. Existing declarations/outcomes
retain their original builds/limits. New frozen builds/protocols need separate
series; no pooling, slot replacement or automatic retry after failure.
The canonical validator is
[resident-benchmark.ts](../../src/evaluation/resident-benchmark.ts), not a
second DTO schema in documentation.

## Timing and acceptance

At **20 Hz** during healthy native tick progress, capture-to-applied-input
**p95 ≤50 ms**, **p99 ≤100 ms**, with **no infrastructure-caused gap ≥1,000 ms**
while the valid fixture has input ready. Lower rates are exploratory and still
subject to load independence. Expiry is not continuity. Keep maxima, missed
slots, expiries, stalls, unconfirmed/censored decisions and clock uncertainty.

Before admission, after asynchronous admission/before warmup, and before full
measurement, require the entire remaining window to fit both original root and
slot work cutoff. **Equality refuses**; cleanup cannot become measurement.
Refusal consumes the slot and reports required/available budgets; do not shorten
windows or grant another attempt. Timer notifications alone do not establish
elapsed warmup/wait/load time; recheck original monotonic deadlines.

Capture native request spans and absolute scheduler traces from source-bound
subscription through Stop and actual native/controller-context drain. Retain
stream refs and integrity: accepted/refused counts, sequences/anchors, observer
errors, actual endpoints and journal completeness. Complete capture of a partial
lifecycle is not a full-window pass. Missing native stages/clock correspondence
stay unknown; actual applied ACKs retain input authority.

Measured-frame populations include late confirmed ACKs arriving after the
window. Join those ACKs only inside original root/work deadlines before planned
evaluator Stop; after synchronous population reads, recheck deadline/abort/native
scope immediately before Stop. Never delay operator/authority Stop. Post-window
submissions cancelled at Stop stay unconfirmed/unknown and outside the fixed
measured population. Conditional quantiles cannot imply omitted decisions passed.

Observer Stop fences immediately and joins acquisition, steps and native-close
owners. A stopping callback emits no fresh sample/step/lease renewal/result.
Previously dispatched inputs/later ACKs stay attributable.

## Closure and media

Join actual resident retirement before tearing down its native fixture/channel.
Unknown resident drain stays unknown despite later containment. Reader evidence
includes query start/end/error, actual child exit/pipe drain and cleanup before
Store close. A pre-fixture refusal can prove no native owner acquired; once
created, confirmed closure requires that native report. Dead PID/timeout/kill
requests never substitute for drain.

Only after **all** native owners close, derive sealed replays: at most
**30 seconds per encoder**, clipped to the **original cohort deadline**.
Derivatives are separate from immutable child outcomes; missing/failed media
prevents technical cohort completion. Full original-speed inspection remains
required. Technical success has `agent_status:"not_run"` and null goal/usage,
never agent completion or human-play approval.
