# Evaluation authority and attempt policy

Ordinary play uses the developer-written Java player and asynchronous advice
from a configured model. Product support is model-agnostic; permission for this
coding assistant's development runs belongs to
[development authorization](../development/model-runs.md), not product defaults.
Each owned attempt still needs finite wall/request/artifact bounds, truthful
accounting and actual closure. Current permission cannot change frozen studies
or historical outcomes.

[Storage and finite closure](storage.md) defines separate roots, protected
evidence and the original launcher deadline through final publication.

## Select the boundary that answers the question

| Tier | Establishes | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit | Deterministic parsing, schemas, policy/accounting/classification | Storage, isolation, game effects, model behavior |
| Integration | Real storage/runtime/sandbox protocol at actual dependency seams | Native mechanics or natural-task efficacy |
| Native game | Actual Minecraft, production isolation, zero inference; disclose synthetic callers | Model strategy or ordinary gameplay competence |
| Agent acceptance | Actual selected model and natural task through production interfaces | General improvement, release readiness or causal savings from one episode |

Workers do not run checks/providers/services during edits. After handoff the
owner runs the smallest affected check; relevant source work gets one combined
final regression/build. Docs-only work needs no game/source build. Rebuild Java/
UI only when inputs changed. Diagnose a reported failure without rerunning it
merely to confirm the report.

Classify platform, agent, provider and semantic goal outcomes independently,
with reasons. A failed goal alone is not a platform fault; successful play cannot
excuse broken isolation/evidence. Authorized image/observation preparation
failure is a platform fault even before the first request or after settled
requests. `runtime.model_observation_failed` admits no request and cannot
reclassify a settled provider result.

## Durable attempt ownership

Before inference freeze purpose/hypothesis or immutable study slot, selected
model, platform-evidence revision, finite limits, task/verifier and configuration/
source provenance. Reserve atomically before auth/native preparation; gate each
request before transport. Interrupted/unresolved reservations remain counted
across restarts and output directories. Settle independent outcomes, usage and
evidence; resource-drain confirmation is a separate durable fact.

Unknown consumption differs from unknown ownership. A completed outcome, dead
PID, terminal SDK event or kill request proves no drain. Join the actual stream
iterator and Agent/turn/auth scopes, workers/native processes and writers before
closing Store/ledger. Later cleanup cannot rewrite an interrupted outcome.
Unknown owners retain capacity and close admissions.

Interruption cancels postprocessing, durably interrupts unsettled reservations,
retains observed goal proof in `interrupted_case`, prevents later normal
settlement/another case and joins original work. Do not start a replacement
renderer. Kill an aborted encoder and await actual process close; retain sealed
capture/index/timeline. Storage failure still closes recorder/journal handles.

Acceptance and native recording use supported SDK postmortem shutdown:
SIGINT/SIGTERM exits 130/143, ten-second SDK hard deadline, at most nine seconds
for owned cleanup—not 25 seconds per resource. Reports begin with cleanup
unconfirmed; expiry stays unconfirmed. Resident slots register before their
first await through durable final publication; signals fence immediately with
one non-renewable horizon ≤9 seconds clipped to original deadlines and one
second inside it reserved for publication. A signal after outcome commit still
joins persistence; parent records cohort interruption separately.

## Retained diagnostic policy: general harness only

Historical generic policy grants `gpt-6-luna` unlimited cumulative finite
diagnostic/gameplay attempts without a new hypothesis/revision or per-run
approval. Non-Luna diagnostics stop after two unsuccessful attempts on the same
hypothesis/evidence revision absent concrete new platform evidence. Model,
prompt, directory or conversation changes do not reset that count. At most two
unresolved diagnostic reservations share a hypothesis. More expensive models
require permission unless already authorized; no silent model fallback.

**That restrictive non-Luna rule is retained general-harness policy, not a
product-wide model restriction.** Current coding-assistant authorization is
separate and cannot reopen exhausted historical comparisons or alter allocations.

Evidence revisions preserve prior failures, state a reason and bind concrete
source/schema corrections, native artifacts or reproducible platform results.
The ledger rejects hashes matching initial or previous revisions; moved reports/
restored old bytes are not novelty. Typed reproduction findings bind commands and
hash-matched support; schemas cannot prove causal attribution. Settle/interrupt
in-flight work before a revision. Accounting-only qualification grants no new
revision or attempt.

## Frozen studies

Before first inference declare identity/question, arms, source/model/profile/
helper/library identities, task/verifier, initial-state groups, sample/order,
per-attempt/cohort/request/concurrency/wall limits, interventions, analysis and
stopping/repetition rules. Reserve exact `(study,arm,initial_state,repetition)`
slots durably. Preserve every scheduled failure, interruption, unknown and
pre-perturbation failure. No retrospective diagnostic-to-study conversion.

Stop for safety, compromised authority, leakage, exhausted bounds or an
invalidating platform defect. Ordinary gameplay failure is an outcome, not a
reason to truncate a valid fixed cohort. Changes to frozen harness/prompts/
helpers/model settings/inherited library/scoring after outcomes close that cohort
for its original claim. Retain it as development evidence and register a new
comparison using appropriate untouched initial states. Expansion/repetition
needs a rule declared before outcomes; no automatic replacement attempts.

Whole-agent/acquisition studies may permit run-private model repair under frozen
authority/cost rules; seal it from other slots and promotion until batch closure.
Frozen-controller assays keep their named controller fixed. A research plan is
not inference permission or a new diagnostic alias.

The current whole-agent route admits cold fresh seeded Normal Survival natural
tasks, private Store/programs and no global promotion. It rejects inherited,
restored/prepared, alternative/frozen controllers and Jev before preparation.
Admission-only mode has no game/provider and rejects external-resource flags.
The original reference has its [own entry](released-reference.md).

Before research activation-semantics edits, the parent owns a deterministic
real Fabric/Docker/Runtime/Store planning baseline with disclosed synthetic
strategist and real isolated guard; a prepared fixture/source inspection cannot
open that edit gate. The historical eight-case manual/automatic baseline met
its source-cohort prerequisite after caller-hook repair, not a changed protocol
or resident/gameplay-improvement acceptance.
