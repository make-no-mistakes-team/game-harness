four additional distant logs and returned alive on the surface. Independent
receipts, observations, stopped save and footage supported all six clauses and
both worksite returns. The leftover initial setup log was excluded from gains.
The operator accepted the uncut recording; there is no automatic custom verifier.

empty inventory/fresh working experience, historical
`minecraft_assisted_rgb_360_r8_v1`, balanced motor, visible inventory and
single-dispatch SSE. Prospective caps: **30 admissions,360,000 ms gameplay,
900,000 ms total**. Actual **203.44 seconds**, seven logs retained;
`task_finish`, platform pass/provider ok/custom model-reported-unverified.

All **6,082 delivered frames** were retained—not every render; capture/state
were unsynchronized and audio absent. All request/native owners closed; earlier
failed attempts stayed retained.

avoiding prior terminal `output_limit`. Local walking helpers and two revisions

Two partially applied navigation unknowns were fenced/reconciled before new
