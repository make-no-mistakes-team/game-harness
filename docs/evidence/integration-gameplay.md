# Integration evidence: gameplay cohorts

These are historical **model-authored GameHarness** episodes, not proof of the
ordinary Java Survival player's combined diamonds/Nether target.
Each result retains its original world, assistance and interface conditions.
No cohort below supports a controlled efficiency, invoice, cost-per-success,
full I01, real Jev or release-readiness claim. Known cost was null unless stated.

## Reviewed two-trip task, 27 October 2026

Sol used explicit low reasoning, fresh seed-1 Normal Survival, empty inventory,
fresh programs/experience, assisted observation, balanced motors, visible
inventory mechanics and single-dispatch SSE. Independent receipts, saved state,
observations and uncut 1× footage supported all six custom clauses:
establish a table worksite; gather two new logs at least six horizontal blocks
away and return; gather four more on a second trip; return alive on the surface
within reach with at least six. Final inventory held seven logs.

The accepted 203.44-second clip retained 6,082/6,082 delivered frames with
upstream gaps disclosed. There were 11 admissions, 100.825 seconds in request
spans and an 18.604-second longest action-free gap; cleanup was confirmed.
Runtime status remained `model_reported_unverified`, not an installed verifier.
It was not an improvement over the earlier 198.88-second episode.
Two partial-navigation unknowns were fenced/reconciled, never replayed.
Walking helpers were reused, but the second gathering procedure was newly
authored despite prior source/arguments/outcome availability.
Changed-quantity procedure reuse and local refusal recovery were unproved.
