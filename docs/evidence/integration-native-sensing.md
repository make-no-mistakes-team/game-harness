vanilla block/entity/miss crosshair; GHV2 pose join after full turns;

559→556 with unchanged observation count. The inventory observation settled

remained accepted. The then-final bare suite passed 1,266 tests.

selected fluid, vehicle, priming and worker GUI frames were inspected.
There was no audio. Mute/attenuation negatives had predicate proofs, not
live mute-test credit. Resident streaming, expanded visibility, multipart
combat attribution and autonomous quality were not established.

## Historical v6 dynamic sensing, 9 November 2026

v6 retained an **eight-block, camera-independent** assisted range.

terrain cache. Candidate cap 128 preceded visibility filtering; at most 32
permitted rows were returned. Coverage stayed partial without rejected counts;
terrain retained its own sequence/time and one-second stale threshold.
These results do not qualify v7's changed camera-bound perception.

Optics admitted air/open water/bubble columns but blocked waterlogged solids,
lava, unknown cells and opaque walls. Vanilla invisibility/world/distance
checks still applied. Visible bounds/aim points were geometry, not health
or intent. Neutralizing a producer invalidated dependent cache/target pins.

Mechanical feedback distinguished consumed attack keys, callback entry and
actual damage/kill. Coarse heard sounds carried no source identity.

same-tick use release/attack could consume input without callback.
These are instrumented mechanics, not host combat strategy.

one measured semantic duration was 1,239.87 ms.

producers stopped; late renewal failed.

passed; world-input and stream cohorts exercised 28 and 12 cases.

## v7 camera-bound entities

v7 required an actual entity-renderer witness in a completed framebuffer.
Camera rotation and dispatcher placement were separate pinned-client
transforms. Uncaptured renders could neither erase nor refresh completed-image
evidence; a new completed image replaced it atomically.

Four actual-client cases passed:

- A front entity reached both permitted read paths.
- Turning away removed it; an old-target attack failed without camera/world effects.
- A silent new entity behind the camera stayed hidden until a turn.
- A genuinely played rear sound stayed audible without granting entity geometry.

Java 21 geometry, capture-boundary and sound proofs also passed.
Earlier failed native attempts exposed capture-publication/camera-transform
defects and remain failed, not extra successful repetitions.
This qualifies the exercised boundary, not pixel-only perception,
model-authored combat competence or ordinary-world reliability.
