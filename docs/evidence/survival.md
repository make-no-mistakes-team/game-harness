# Ordinary Survival evidence and unresolved feedback

As of **25 November 2026**, inspected campaigns establish early tools, iron
tools, and partial mining — **not diamonds or Nether entry**. No retained run
establishes both in one fresh Normal Survival campaign. Subsequent v2 engine
changes (modular Java rewrite, shore-cast portal casting, safe high-drop
descent, hardened combat/shelter, cave exploration, and inventory triage) have
unit and prepared-diagnostic coverage, not yet fresh-world combined campaign
proof. Exact model identities and request accounting live in each private run
declaration.

## Recovery checkpoint, 24 November 2026

The initial recovery attempt stopped after **217.180 seconds** with full
health, no inventory or broken rock, and **zero model requests**. The user
observed canopy refusal and repeated quarter-turn motion. Source inspection
traced this to cardinal route edges driving camera heading — a local-player
defect, not a test of model reasoning.

Later continuation footage showed nonfatal descents costing one and two health
points. All three fatal encounters in that attempt were explicitly attributed
to zombies.

The ordinary path then collected wood and crafted a wooden pickaxe about
**16 seconds after player readiness**, followed by a wooden shovel. The client
crashed at the first-stone transition while the world and tools survived. After
that crash fix, another continuation dug **36 dirt and no cobblestone**,
repeatedly descending three blocks and climbing back up.

The user reviewed a **33.2-second, 1× excerpt** of the wood/tools attempt:
movement was broadly acceptable, but **00:18–00:30** still showed standing with
slow, interrupted cursor motion. That interval remains rejected.

A temporary, explicitly assisted ore-coordinate sequence reached three raw
iron, smelted three ingots, and crafted an iron pickaxe through ordinary
actions. A continuation escaped a flooded mine after restart and descended to
`y = 39`, later lost its paid-charcoal worksite during stall recovery, and
stopped without diamonds. A subsequent eight-block fall killed the 0.4-health
player; the next life died in surface combat without recovering gear. Ore
assistance precludes any unassisted campaign claim.

Prepared 16-block bucket trials verified pre-landing water placement, undamaged
landing, and safe source reclaim: drop trial (placement tick 130, wet contact
132, reclaim 142, settled footing 155, min health 20) and run-up trial (takeoff
tick 124, wet contact 153, settled 166, min health 20, 1 jump, 82 cm sprint).
A prepared berry trial verified harvesting, pickup, eating, and natural healing
(two berries eaten, health 4 → ~5.8, hunger 19, zero deaths, clean shutdown).
None of these prepared trials establishes ordinary campaign competence or 1×
motion approval.

## Review and acceptance

Scored footage uses 1× speed with no internal cuts or overlays, startup
reported separately, and independent judgments for task verification and
purposeful rhythm. Remaining acceptance demands diamonds **and** Nether entry
in one fresh campaign, purposeful local play independent of advice, natural
off-center aim and continuous travel, combat/shelter reflexes, and reliable
food/healing within the [perception boundary](../survival/perception.md).
