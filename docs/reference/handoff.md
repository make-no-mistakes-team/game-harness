# Playing handoff and speculative successors

These generic GameHarness mechanisms preserve a useful incumbent while a model prepares another immutable program. They supply no gameplay strategy, state migration or effect replay.

## Exact ordinary replacement

Submit/start can name `handoff` with the exact latest predecessor name/version/execution ordinal. Omission never replaces live work. Validate successor source, entry, limits and policy before disturbing the incumbent.

Then fence new capabilities throughout the predecessor tree. An admitted semantic leaf action/read may settle under original authority; retire inside its completion boundary before returning control to source. A waiting child does not get to finish its remaining algorithm. Resident predecessors and trees without admitted leaves retire immediately.

Join actual workers and native retirement, bracketed by authorized observations, then admit the exact successor without another model request between stop/start. Stop, tool/turn abort and root expiry revoke immediately rather than waiting for graceful handoff.

If the exact predecessor naturally succeeded during authoring, confirmed same-scope retirement can still permit handoff. Failed/blocked/cancelled/unknown/superseded predecessors refuse. Task/generation/life/boot/space/screen changes, newer work, deliberate Stop or unconfirmed cleanup block admission. Duplicate acknowledged calls return their original selection without cancelling the successor; interrupted delivery cannot redispatch.

## Resident preparation

Pin exact source bytes/hash, dependency closure, request, scope, predecessor, intent version and original deadline before retirement. A disposable isolated shadow activation checks dynamic applicability; discard its state/events/globals. After confirmed closure, a **separate clean worker** compiles without invocation and must be ready while incumbent still runs.

Only one successor preparation is owned. Startup has its finite bound; ready retention ends at predecessor deadline. Cancellation/source/intent/scope change discards and joins candidate cleanup. Consume the clean worker once only after predecessor retirement. Its first live activation receives fresh post-retirement frame and rechecks authority after computation; no probe result is replayed. Deadline is no later than predecessor's or requested duration. This path does not invoke a separate game-guard worker.

## Explicit lookahead

Main opts into one flight/draft in root-start `next`, up to 256 opt-ins/run. Delay is 0–600,000 ms; flight permits 1–4 calls, 1–16 tools and 1,000–30,000 ms. That duration is one total deadline for preparation, staging, predecessor wait, fresh guard and admission. No correction or closure renews it.

Ordinary decisions take priority and all strategic requests remain serialized. Omission makes no call/permit; retry does not rearm. The speculative role may observe, inspect/read help, save inactive source and stage one exact candidate. It cannot play, activate, assess outcomes, mutate reflex/memory or mint continuation authority.

`prepare_next` defaults run; explicit step selects resident limits/state. Intent is authored args; no predecessor live stack, held inputs or lease transfers. Changed predecessor intent invalidates staged continuation. Source/closure/compatibility hashes stay pinned and are rechecked around awaits.

Optional guard is an async body with only fresh authorized observation and args, returning strict boolean. Omission records omitted/null hash, not passed. Supplied guard runs statically validated and isolated readonly; false, nonboolean or error never grants effects. It is a model gameplay condition, not a substitute for host safety.

Main alone chooses manual (default) or on-predecessor-success activation and mints one scoped permit. Automatic activation waits for both settled validated staging and successful drained predecessor, in either order. One host task consumes the permit, reobserves, checks guard and uses ordinary durable admission; no extra activation inference races the outcome wake.

Pause/Stop/death/task/session/generation changes, unsuccessful predecessor, source change or expiry revoke. Rejection retains evidence and wakes ordinary planning once, without retry, alternative or unknown-effect replay. Whitelisted guard validation corrections share original flight bounds; unsafe source/stale authority remain terminal.

Planning projections retain candidate, guard omission/result, permit/mode/trigger, observation, predecessor closure and activation evidence. Measured overlap is descriptive, not causal speedup.
